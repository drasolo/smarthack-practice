# Jury questions

1. Read `pitch/judge-qa.md`, CONTEXT.md > Decisions and `evals/results/latest.md`.
2. For each question, write a two-sentence answer grounded in what we built: numbers from the
   eval table, decisions with their reasons. No claims the demo can't back.
3. Rehearse: ask the human the questions one at a time. After each answer, say in one line what
   was weak or missing.
4. Add every new question that comes up to `pitch/judge-qa.md`.
